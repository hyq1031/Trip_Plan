Day by day

01

Bayside, Art & Sunset Penguins

Wed 12 Aug South Melbourne → St Kilda — Myki day

The one day you leave the Free Tram Zone — load a Myki card before you head out.

Morning

Route 96 tram to South Melbourne Market — closed Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays, so Wednesday works. Breakfast pastries at Agathé Pâtisserie.

Midday

Tram to the NGV International on St Kilda Road — walk under the water wall entrance, the stained-glass ceiling, the permanent collection is free.

Afternoon

Tram further south to St Kilda — the palm-lined Esplanade, Acland Street cake shops, a photo at the old Luna Park gate.

Sunset / Evening

St Kilda Pier Breakwater at dusk for the wild little penguin colony — free. Dinner at Hotel Esplanade (The Espy).

02

Laneways, Coffee & Melbourne Museum

Thu 13 Aug CBD → Carlton — free tram/walk

Back inside the Free Tram Zone — today costs close to nothing to get around.

Morning

Coffee at Degraves Street or Centre Place, then the graffiti lanes — Hosier Lane and AC/DC Lane. State Library Victoria for the domed La Trobe Reading Room.

Lunch

Free tram north to Lygon Street, Carlton — Italian at Brunetti Classico.

Afternoon

Melbourne Museum in Carlton Gardens — the Bunjilaka Aboriginal Cultural Centre, the Forest Gallery, and the UNESCO-listed Royal Exhibition Building right outside.

Evening

Free tram back to the CBD. Southbank Promenade along the Yarra, then Melbourne Skydeck (Eureka Tower) for sunset 360° views.

03

Museums of Federation Square & Old Melbourne

Fri 14 Aug CBD — free tram/walk

Three museums, all free or near enough, all inside the tram zone.

Morning

ACMI (Australian Centre for the Moving Image) at Federation Square — interactive film, TV and video game galleries, the centrepiece exhibition is free.

Lunch

Federation Square, or a sandwich on Flinders Lane.

Afternoon

Immigration Museum on Flinders Street, then Old Melbourne Gaol on Russell Street — the cell Ned Kelly was held in.

Evening

Craft cocktails behind the bookcase at Eau De Vie, or the shipping-container bar Section 8.

If you'd rather trade this day: Great Ocean Road

A full-day guided coach tour from the CBD replaces this whole day rather than adding to it — a professional driver handles the cliffside roads while you watch the Twelve Apostles, Loch Ard Gorge, and wild koalas at Kennett River go by. No driving needed, but it's paid and takes the full 12 hours.

04

Gardens, MCG & Fitzroy Farewell

Sat 15 Aug CBD East → Fitzroy — tram (Myki)

Last day — MCG in the morning, a farewell dinner to close it out.

Morning

Fitzroy Gardens — Cook's Cottage and the Conservatory.

Midday

MCG tour, plus the Australian Sports Museum inside the ground.

Afternoon

Tram to Fitzroy, Brunswick Street — bookshops, vintage stores, street art.

Evening

Farewell dinner at Chin Chin, Flinders Lane.

If you'd rather trade this day: Puffing Billy & kangaroos

Belgrave Line train from Flinders Street to Belgrave (1hr), then the historic Puffing Billy steam train through the mountain-ash forest. Train back, then Bus 905 to Westerfolds Park — arrive two hours before sunset for wild Eastern Grey Kangaroos on the open grassland.

Getting around

Free in the centre, cheap everywhere else

Every tram inside the CBD grid and Docklands is free — no tap on, no tap off, as long as your whole trip stays inside that zone. Step outside it (St Kilda, Brighton, Carlton, Fitzroy) and you need a physical Myki card ($6 AUD) loaded with Myki Money — tap on boarding trams, tap on and off at train gates.

Only Day 1 needs the Myki card.

Daily fares cap around $10.60 AUD — after your first two trips of the day, everything else (train, tram, bus) is free for the rest of that day. From the airport, the SkyBus runs straight to Southern Cross Station (~20–30 min, ~$22 AUD one-way) — there's no local train from Tullamarine.

Where to stay

Stay inside the Free Tram Zone

CBD, the Flinders Street corridor, or Southbank — everything on this itinerary is walkable or a free tram ride from here, and it's also the SkyBus drop-off point. The three shortlists below are each ranked for value, comfort, and location, all within or right on the edge of that same zone.

Best value — top 5

  1. 01YHA Melbourne Central — Flinders Street, hostel with private rooms as well as dorms, unbeatable Free Tram Zone location.
  2. 02Space Hotel — hybrid hostel/hotel near Carlton Gardens, close to Melbourne Museum.
  3. 03Nomads Melbourne — hostel on Hardware Lane, right in the laneway core.
  4. 04ibis Styles Melbourne The Victoria Hotel — budget chain on Little Collins Street, CBD.
  5. 05Metro Hotel Marlow Melbourne — budget CBD hotel near Queen Victoria Market.

Most comfortable — top 5

  1. 01The Ritz-Carlton Melbourne — Flinders Street west end, luxury bedding and service, city and bay views.
  2. 02Grand Hyatt Melbourne — Collins Street, spacious rooms, full-service comfort.
  3. 03Crown Towers Melbourne — Southbank, high-end comfort, river views.
  4. 04The Langham Melbourne — Southbank, renowned for its spa and plush rooms.
  5. 05QT Melbourne — Russell Street, boutique-design hotel, well reviewed comfort in the CBD core.

Most convenient location — top 5

  1. 01The Quincy Hotel — Flinders Lane, dead-center CBD, inside the Free Tram Zone.
  2. 02Ovolo Laneways — Little Bourke Street, in the heart of the laneways.
  3. 03Grand Hyatt Melbourne — Collins Street, inside the Free Tram Zone, close to Flinders Street Station.
  4. 04Crowne Plaza Melbourne — near Flinders Street Station and Southbank, both tram and train access.
  5. 05Holiday Inn Melbourne on Flinders — right on Flinders Street, next to the station and the Free Tram Zone boundary.

These are shortlisted by reputation and location, not live pricing — I haven't verified current rates. Confirm actual prices and availability on Booking.com or Google Hotels before booking.

Where the money actually goes

  • Free museums — NGV International's permanent galleries, the State Library, and ACMI's main exhibitions all have free general admission.
  • Skip Phillip Island — the wild little penguin colony at St Kilda Pier is free and a local tram ride away, instead of $150+ AUD tours to Phillip Island.
  • Ride the transit cap — after two trips outside the Free Tram Zone, the rest of that day's trams, trains and buses are free.
