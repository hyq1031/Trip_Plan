Day 1: Historic Lanes, Coffee & Melbourne Museum

Free Tram Grid
Morning

Laneway Coffee & Street Art Crawl

Start your Melbourne journey in the heart of the city's alleyways. Grab coffee in Degraves Street or Centre Place, then marvel at the world-famous street art in Hosier Lane. Walk up Swanston Street to visit the magnificent State Library Victoria and take photos of the stunning La Trobe Reading Room dome.

Coffee: Degraves Espresso Bar Photo: La Trobe Dome Street Art: Hosier Lane
Afternoon

Melbourne Museum & Carlton Gardens

Walk or take the free tram north to Carlton. Indulge in wood-fired pizza and desserts at Brunetti Classico. Afterwards, spend the afternoon exploring the Melbourne Museum in Carlton Gardens, featuring the Bunjilaka Aboriginal Cultural Centre and the Forest Gallery. View the historic UNESCO World Heritage-listed Royal Exhibition Building right outside.

Museum: Melbourne Museum Lunch: Brunetti Classico (Lygon St) View: Royal Exhibition Building
Evening

Southbank Promenade Walk & Skydeck Sunset

Return to the CBD via the free tram. Cross the Yarra River to the Southbank Promenade. Ride the elevator to the top of the Melbourne Skydeck (Eureka Tower) for the highest 360-degree sunset view in the city. Finish with dumplings in Chinatown (Little Bourke St) or floating drinks at Ponyfish Island.

Sunset: Melbourne Skydeck (88th Floor) Dinner: Shanghai Street (Chinatown)

Day 2: Bayside Vibe, Stained-Glass Art & Sunset Penguins

Myki Card Required
Morning

South Melbourne Market Exploration

Take the Route 96 tram to the historic South Melbourne Market. Browse artisan cheese, local crafts, and order fresh French croissants at Agathé Pâtisserie or sample the legendary local Dim Sims.

Shop: South Melbourne Market Pastry: Agathé Pâtisserie Note: Market closed Mon/Tue/Thu
Afternoon

NGV International & Brighton Bathing Boxes

Visit the National Gallery of Victoria (NGV International) on St Kilda Road. See international masterpieces and walk under the grand water wall. Afterwards, board the Sandringham train to Middle Brighton and walk 15 mins to Dendy Street Beach to view the 82 iconic, colorful historic Bathing Boxes.

Art: NGV International (Free) Beach: Brighton Bathing Boxes
Evening

St Kilda Esplanade & Pier Breakwater Penguins

Tram to St Kilda. Stroll down the palm-lined Esplanade, view Luna Park, and walk to the end of St Kilda Pier breakwater at dusk. Watch the wild colony of Little Penguins return to shore. End the night with dinner at the iconic Hotel Esplanade (The Espy).

Wildlife: Wild Little Penguins (Free!) Dinner: The Espy (Hotel Esplanade)

Day 3: Interactive Moving Images & Historic Gaols

Free Tram Grid
Day Trip Alternative Available: Skip this day in the city to do the full-day Great Ocean Road Guided Coach Tour. No driving required; passenger coach picks up in CBD. (Check the box in the sidebar budget calculator to update totals).
Morning

ACMI Moving Image Exhibition

Visit the ACMI (Australian Centre for the Moving Image) at Federation Square. Immerse yourself in the interactive galleries celebrating film, TV, digital art, and videogame history (permanent exhibition is free and highly engaging).

Museum: ACMI (Federation Square) Cost: Free Entry
Afternoon

Immigration Museum & Old Melbourne Gaol

Visit the Immigration Museum on Flinders Street to explore rich stories of immigration and identity. Afterwards, head up to the historic Old Melbourne Gaol on Russell Street to step inside the 19th-century cells where Ned Kelly was held.

Museum: Immigration Museum History: Old Melbourne Gaol
Evening

Melbourne Hidden Speakeasy Crawl

Unwind with a craft cocktail hidden behind the bookcase at Eau De Vie on Malthouse Lane, or grab a craft beer at the industrial outdoor shipping container bar Section 8 in Tattersalls Lane.

Cocktail: Eau De Vie Vibe: Section 8 (Lane Bar)

Day 4: Botanic Gardens, Sports Heritage & Fitzroy Crawl

Myki Card Required
Day Trip Alternative Available: Skip this day in the city to take the metropolitan train to Belgrave for the Puffing Billy Steam Railway and Westerfolds Park wild kangaroos. No driving required.
Morning

Fitzroy Gardens & Australian Sports Museum

Stroll through Fitzroy Gardens to see Cook's Cottage and the Conservatory. Head to the Melbourne Cricket Ground (MCG) and visit the interactive Australian Sports Museum inside the stadium, showcasing Australia's sports history.

Museum: Australian Sports Museum (MCG) Sight: Cook's Cottage (Fitzroy Gardens)
Afternoon

Brunswick Street Fitzroy Exploration

Take a tram to Fitzroy, Melbourne's hipster heart. Walk down Brunswick Street to browse indie bookshops, vintage clothing boutiques, and explore alleyways covered in colorful murals.

Shopping: Vintage boutiques District: Brunswick St & Fitzroy
Evening

Flinders Lane Farewell Dinner

Finish your trip with a culinary feast along Flinders Lane. Experience the electric energy and Southeast Asian flavors of Chin Chin, or enjoy seasonal, modern Australian dining at Cumulus Inc.

Dinner: Chin Chin (Flinders Lane) Alternative: Cumulus Inc.

Best Money Value Strategies

Free Tram Zone Hack

Keep your travel within the CBD grid and you pay $0. Only tap your Myki card when heading out of the CBD (like to St Kilda or Fitzroy).

St Kilda Penguins (Free)

Phillip Island penguin tours cost $150+ AUD. Seeing the wild penguin colony at St Kilda Pier breakwater is 100% free and costs only a tram ticket.

Free Galleries & Exhibits

Entry to the National Gallery of Victoria (NGV) and ACMI (moving image museum at Federation Square) is completely free for general entry.
